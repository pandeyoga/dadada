Research date: 2026-11-23. Official pages (rumahningrat.com, rnliving.rumahningrat.com, rumahningratgold.com, trusmigroup.com, Google Play, YouTube, TikTok) were fetched directly on this date. ## 1. Entity disambiguation: which "Rumah Ningrat" is it, and which one has an app?

### Takeaway
"Rumah Ningrat" is, for practical purposes, a single business: the housing-developer brand of **PT Raja Sukses Propertindo**, the property unit of the Cirebon-based **Trusmi Group** (Ibnu Riyanto, Batik Trusmi). It was called **Trusmiland** until a 2023 rebrand. This is the entity with an app: **"Keluarga Ningrat"**, an app for residents published by "Trusmi Group" (Android `com.trusmi.keluarganingrat`, iOS id6752388007). A separate buyer and sales tool called "Ningrat Apps" also exists. I found no other active business (agent, interior, homestay, restaurant, fashion) trading as "Rumah Ningrat". The other matches are a generic cultural term, a café run by the same developer, and an unrelated Bali spa.

## 2. What the business sells: products, projects, prices, target customers and coverage

### Takeaway
Rumah Ningrat builds and sells mostly **government-subsidised landed houses (rumah subsidi, type 30/60)** for low-to-middle-income first-time buyers. It also sells some commercial (non-subsidised) houses and shophouses (ruko). Its base is the **Ciayumajakuning region of West Java** (Cirebon, Indramayu, Majalengka, Kuningan), and it has expanded to Subang, **Bekasi**, **Kendal** (Central Java) and, from late 2026, **Cikampek/Karawang** under the "Rumah Ningrat Gold" sub-brand. Current list prices are about Rp150–185 million, with instalments of about Rp1 million per month. Every unit comes bundled with a free fence and rear wall, CCTV and the resident app.

## 3. Legal entity: PT name, registration, founding year and headquarters

The operating company is **PT Raja Sukses Propertindo** (RSP), part of the Trusmi Group holding in Kabupaten Cirebon. ## 5. Business model and scale indicators

### Takeaway
The business is a volume developer of subsidised housing that relies on bank KPR, chiefly **BTN**, the state housing bank. It competes on bundled extras (fence, wall, CCTV, app) and a digital buying journey (online booking, BI-checking, KPR tracking, affiliate referrals). Self-reported scale: **about 200 ha developed, 15,000 units developed, more than 10,000 units sold, and 25, 32 or 45+ projects** depending on the page. External validation is limited to BTN awards and a reported BTN ranking of #2 nationally for subsidised sales in 2022.

### Inferences
- At about 10,000 units sold and roughly Rp150–185M per unit, cumulative sales value would be on the order of Rp1.5–1.8 trillion over about 13 years. This is a rough estimate, not a reported figure.
- BTN dependence is strong: the awards, the BTN Properti listings and the BTN Syariah award all point to BTN as the main KPR channel. The sharia link is only an award name, not a documented "tanpa bank" scheme.
- The heavy investment in proprietary software (booking, BI-check, AI QC dashboard, resident app with security, marketplace and IPL payment) is a deliberate differentiator. It also makes Rumah Ningrat a sophisticated buyer or competitor for property-development software.

### Gaps

- I found no list of partner banks beyond BTN (and BTN Syariah, by award name). The homepage's "Bank Partner" section did not render as text.
