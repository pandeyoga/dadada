# Rumah Ningrat (Trusmiland / PT Raja Sukses Propertindo): Reputation, Market Position and Digital Strategy

Research date: 2026-11-23. Scope: perception (customers, media, regulators), risks and controversies, competitive positioning, and digital stack compared with typical Indonesian developer tools. Corporate profile and app-store metadata are covered by other researchers and are only touched on here where needed.

**Identity (needed context).** "Rumah Ningrat" is the consumer brand of a Cirebon-based **subsidized-housing (rumah subsidi / KPR FLPP) developer**. Its projects are mostly named "Trusmiland [location]", and the developer entity named in news and government listings is **PT Raja Sukses Propertindo (PT RSP)**, whose owner is **Ibnu Riyanto**. It is a property developer, so the comparison with the user's property-development OS applies directly.
- The "Rumah Ningrat" site calls itself "Perumahan Subsidi oleh TrusmiLand", and its WordPress blog posts are authored by "Trusmiland" — [punyarumah.rumahningrat.com (search title)](https://punyarumah.rumahningrat.com/login); [wp.rumahningrat.com author page](https://wp.rumahningrat.com/author/admin/)
- The government's Sikumbang/Tapera listing names PT RAJA SUKSES PROPERTINDO as the developer of Trusmiland Klayan IV Tahap II — [Sikumbang Tapera](https://sikumbang.tapera.go.id/lokasi-perumahan/SBR2120052021T001)
- Radar Cirebon (11 Jun 2026) calls Ibnu Riyanto the owner of PT RSP, developer of Perumahan Trusmiland Klayan — [Radar Cirebon](https://radarcirebon.disway.id/kabupaten-cirebon/read/222546/murka-ibnu-riyanto-jalan-ke-proyek-trusmiland-klayan-diportal-sepihak-melanggar-hukum)
- The Rumah Ningrat brand name does not appear in any of the news coverage of disputes. Those articles say "Trusmiland" or "Trusmi Land" and "PT RSP". Anyone searching only for "Rumah Ningrat" will therefore miss the controversies below.

## 1. Customer sentiment (reviews, social media, forums, video)

### Takeaway
I found almost no independent customer reviews of the brand online: no retrievable Google Maps rating, no Kaskus or Reddit threads, and no critical YouTube or TikTok reviews. The strongest customer-sentiment signal is negative and well documented. Residents of **Trusmiland Klayan Tahap 5** (Kab. Cirebon) report recurring floods since 2023. Hundreds signed a petition, they escalated to the DPRD and the Bupati in Sept 2026, and two mediations with the developer failed. Positive signals mostly come from the developer's own content and from local promo creators.

### Cited Findings
**Negative / complaints (independent news, dated):**
- **11 Sep 2026:** About 30 residents of Perumahan Trusmiland Klayan Tahap 5 (Desa Battembat, Kec. Tengahtani) went to DPRD Kab. Cirebon with a petition signed by "hundreds of residents". Floods have occurred since 2023, before the estate was officially occupied. In the late-2025 to early-2026 rainy season water reached about 0.5 m on roads and inside homes. Blocks R and S were the worst hit, hundreds of homes were affected, and some residents evacuated — [Kabarbaru.co, 11 Sep 2026](https://kabarbaru.co/keluhkan-banjir-warga-perumahan-trusmiland-cirebon-ngadu-ke-bupati-dan-minta-dprd-turun-tangan/)
- Resident Ahmad Sufyan: "Mau sampai kapan kami sengsara dengan banjir yang terus berulang setiap tahun." Residents said they were troubled that the developer had not dealt with the flooding promptly — [Kabarbaru.co, 11 Sep 2026](https://kabarbaru.co/keluhkan-banjir-warga-perumahan-trusmiland-cirebon-ngadu-ke-bupati-dan-minta-dprd-turun-tangan/)
- **26 Oct 2026:** Two mediations between residents and the developer (18 Sep and 20 Oct 2026) reached no agreement. Residents say the blocks sit lower than the main road. They want houses and roads raised to the site-plan elevation ("Sesimpel itu keinginan warga" — Yogi, residents' advocacy team) — [Kabarbaru.co, 26 Oct 2026](https://kabarbaru.co/polemik-banjir-perumahan-trusmiland-klayan-5-dprd-kabupaten-cirebon-minta-pengembang-stop-sementara-proyek-pembangunan/); [Alexanews, 26 Oct 2026](https://alexanews.id/2026/10/26/dprd-kabupaten-cirebon-minta-proyek-trusmiland-klayan-5-dihentikan-sementara-banjir-tiga-tahun-tak-tuntas/)
- **Unverified allegation, attributed to a resident:** Sufyan said the developer's owner had once acknowledged a reduction in soil fill ("pengurangan urugan tanah") during early construction and blamed subordinates. The articles carry no developer confirmation — [Kabarbaru.co, 26 Oct 2026](https://kabarbaru.co/polemik-banjir-perumahan-trusmiland-klayan-5-dprd-kabupaten-cirebon-minta-pengembang-stop-sementara-proyek-pembangunan/)
- **Non-buyer neighbours** near the Kec. Sumber (Kelurahan Babakan / Bukit Plangon) project complained of flood risk. Lurah Babakan Budianto said: "Dampaknya jelas, setiap musim hujan warga terancam banjir." — [Ciremai Today, 9 Feb 2026](https://ciremaitoday.com/politik/izin-trusmi-land-terancam-dicabut-dprd-cirebon-soroti-risiko-banjir/)

**Positive / neutral:**
- A third-party local promo account (@aboutcirebonid) posted on TikTok: "Perumahan subsidi di Cirebon yang elegan dan modern secakep ini hanya ada di Trusmiland Klayan" — [TikTok @aboutcirebonid](https://www.tiktok.com/@aboutcirebonid/video/7427621654156627206). The video ID decodes to about 19 Oct 2024. This is likely paid or partner promotion, not an organic review.
- The official TikTok account @rumahningratofficial showed about 31.9K followers and 1.1M likes in a search snippet (undated) — [TikTok @rumahningratofficial](https://www.tiktok.com/@rumahningratofficial)
- The developer's own site claims a "Tingkat Kepuasan Pelanggan Tertinggi" award. That award is listed under the implausible label "Best Selling Non Subsidiez 2028 by Bank BTN", next to an entry that just reads "test" — [rumahningrat.com](https://rumahningrat.com/)

**Ratings and platforms:**
- A Facebook-indexed listing "Rumah Subsidi Trusmiland Cirebon" (Jl. Syekh Datul Kahfi No.1, Plered) showed "Not yet rated (0 reviews)" in an undated snippet — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/inforumahmurahcirebon/)

### Inferences
- The visible complaint pattern centres on **site and engineering quality, especially drainage and land elevation**, not on fraud, refunds or missing certificates. The Klayan 5 dispute moved from residents to DPRD and Bupati within about three years of handover, which suggests the developer's own after-sales and complaint channel did not resolve it.
- The absence of Kaskus or Reddit discussion fits a regional, low-ticket subsidized segment where buyers talk in WhatsApp groups and Facebook groups that search engines don't index. It should not be read as a sign of satisfaction.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve current Google Maps ratings or review counts for the main office (Jl. Haji Abbas, Trusmi, Weru) or the project pins (Klayan, Pamengkang, etc.). A manual check in Google Maps is needed.
- I could not access the comment sections of official TikTok, YouTube or Instagram posts. Facebook groups (e.g., resident groups for Trusmiland Klayan) were not searchable.
- I found no Kaskus or Reddit r/indonesia threads.

## 2. Red flags, disputes and legal or regulatory issues

### Takeaway
I found **no evidence of fraud, unfinished ("mangkrak") projects, refund scandals, YLKI/BPKN complaints, OJK/Satgas PASTI warnings, or consumer wanprestasi verdicts** for Rumah Ningrat, Trusmiland or PT RSP. There are, however, **serious, recent (2026) regulatory and environmental controversies** at the local-legislature level:
1. **Klayan 5:** DPRD Komisi III recommended a temporary construction halt (26 Oct 2026) over three years of flooding and will re-evaluate permits and the site plan.
2. **Kec. Sumber / Bukit Plangon:** DPRD and government agencies linked the project to the 23 Dec 2025 flood of the Pemkab Cirebon office complex. A permit revocation was threatened and reportedly ordered (Feb–Mar 2026), activity reportedly continued, and DPRD issued a stop-work ultimatum with environmental remediation duties (28 Apr 2026).
3. **Village conflicts:** The owner reported alleged pungli by village staff to police (Klayan, Jun 2026), and a road-access blockade and deadlock occurred at Pamengkang (Feb–Mar 2026). In both cases the company is the complainant.

### Cited Findings
**Klayan 5 flooding (Kec. Tengahtani / Gunungjati area):**
- **26 Oct 2026:** Komisi III DPRD Kab. Cirebon, chaired by Anton Maulana, inspected the site and asked for construction to stop temporarily until the flooding is handled. It will send an official letter coordinated with Satpol PP and will evaluate PT RSP's permits and site plan. The developer did not attend despite a formal invitation ("Susah kalau pengembangnya tidak hadir") — [Alexanews, 26 Oct 2026](https://alexanews.id/2026/10/26/dprd-kabupaten-cirebon-minta-proyek-trusmiland-klayan-5-dihentikan-sementara-banjir-tiga-tahun-tak-tuntas/); [Kabarbaru.co, 26 Oct 2026](https://kabarbaru.co/polemik-banjir-perumahan-trusmiland-klayan-5-dprd-kabupaten-cirebon-minta-pengembang-stop-sementara-proyek-pembangunan/)
- Komisi III also pressed for drainage normalization and an embung (retention pond) outside the estate, fed by a sodetan (diversion channel). The developer reportedly agreed to carry out the jointly agreed recommendations — [eSatu.id](https://www.esatu.id/komisi-iii-dprd-kabupaten-cirebon-desak-trusmiland-klayan-5-normalisasi-saluran-dan-bangun-embung/); [InilahKoran](https://www.inilahkoran.id/dprd-kabupaten-cirebon-desak-normalisasi-saluran-dan-pembangunan-embung-di-trusmiland) (from search snippets; not fully read)

- On PSU, DPRD member Diah Irwany Indriyati said that if PSU has not been handed over to the Pemda, the developer is responsible for repairs ("kami akan minta mereka (developer) mempertanggungjawabkan segala sesuatunya") — [Kabarbaru.co, 11 Sep 2026](https://kabarbaru.co/keluhkan-banjir-warga-perumahan-trusmiland-cirebon-ngadu-ke-bupati-dan-minta-dprd-turun-tangan/)

**Kec. Sumber (Kel. Babakan) / Bukit Plangon project:**
- **9 Feb 2026:** DPRD Komisi III chair Anton Maulana said the Trusmi Land permit in Kec. Sumber could be revoked "dalam pekan ini". Several parties suspect the project worsened the **23 Dec 2025** flood that inundated the Pemkab Cirebon office complex, the first such event since the complex was built. Komisi II's Supriyadi: "Izinnya perumahan, tapi yang terjadi justru galian tanah." — [Ciremai Today, 9 Feb 2026](https://ciremaitoday.com/politik/izin-trusmi-land-terancam-dicabut-dprd-cirebon-soroti-risiko-banjir/)

DPRD was "surprised" and planned to summon the developer — [Kabar Cirebon/Pikiran Rakyat](https://kabarcirebon.pikiran-rakyat.com/ciayumajakuning/pr-29310097118/izin-dicabut-proyek-pembanguan-perumahan-trusmiland-di-plangon-tetap-berjalan); [Fajar Cirebon](https://fajarcirebon.com/izin-dicabut-proyek-trusmi-land-masih-jalan-dprd-cirebon-kaget-dan-siap-panggil-pengembang/) (search snippets; full text not retrieved, 403)

**Courts / consumer bodies / OJK:**
- A search of the MA Direktori Putusan surfaced pages where "PT Raja Sukses Propertindo" appears inside exhibits (e.g., audit documents or receipts) of what seem to be other cases. **I found no civil wanprestasi or consumer-dispute verdict naming PT RSP or Trusmiland as a defendant** — [Direktori Putusan MA search "pt.raja"](https://putusan3.mahkamahagung.go.id/search.html/?q=pt.raja); [Direktori Putusan MA search "propertindo"](https://putusan3.mahkamahagung.go.id/search.html?page=17&q=propertindo)
- Targeted searches for fraud, refund, booking-fee or certificate complaints returned **no relevant hits**, only job ads and sales listings. A Nov 2026 certificate-fraud story involves a person named Ariadi and **is not linked** to the developer — [search result: NewsPantau, Nov 2026](https://www.newspantau.com/2026/11/korban-dugaan-penipuan-pengurusan.html)
- **OJK/Satgas PASTI relevance is low.** Sales are financed through bank KPR: the partner portal lists BTN, BNI and Mandiri and shows a bank-KPR simulator, and listings refer to subsidized 5% KPR via partner banks. This is not a bank-less "syariah in-house" scheme — [mitra.rumahningrat.com](https://mitra.rumahningrat.com/product/32-trusmiland-cikoneng-majalengka); [wp.rumahningrat.com Rp<200 juta article](https://wp.rumahningrat.com/rumah-subsidi-di-cirebon-masih-di-bawah-200-juta/)

### Inferences
- The main risk for buyers is **post-handover quality and infrastructure (drainage, elevation, PSU)**, plus **environmental and permit risk on new sites**, which could delay new phases or handovers. These are local-government and DPRD processes, not criminal or consumer-court cases as of Nov 2026.
- The pattern of conflicts with village governments (Klayan and Pamengkang) suggests that land access, community "coordination fees" and social licence are operational pain points for this developer. These are common for regional subsidized developers in Indonesia.
- The developer is absent from DPRD site visits and its own media voice is missing from most articles. That points to a weak public-relations and complaint-handling posture, even though counsel publicly claims good faith.

### Gaps
- I did not query SIPP of PN Sumber (Kab. Cirebon) or PN Cirebon directly, so pending civil cases cannot be excluded.
- I did not check the YLKI/BPKN complaint databases or the OJK Satgas PASTI list directly (no indexed hits).
- The final outcomes are unknown: the official stop-work letter for Klayan 5 (after 26 Oct 2026), whether the Plangon permit revocation was formally issued, and whether remediation (reforestation, rivers, embung) was completed.
- I could not determine whether PSU at Klayan 5 has been handed over to the Pemkab.

## 4. Market position: segment, pricing, USPs, competitors

### Takeaway
Rumah Ningrat is a **high-volume, entry-level subsidized-housing (FLPP) developer** in Ciayumajakuning (Cirebon, Indramayu, Majalengka, Kuningan) with expansion into Subang, Kendal and Bekasi. Typical units are type 30/60, 2 bedrooms, priced at about Rp150–185 million. It differentiates through low entry costs (DP from Rp1 million, booking fee from Rp500 thousand), freebies (fence, back wall), estate security and tech features (one-gate, 24h security, **live CCTV via app**), and "subsidi plus" upgrade options. Its competitors are dozens of small REI/Apersi/Asprumnas subsidized developers offering the same product at the same government-capped price, so differentiation rests on scale, amenities and the resident app rather than on price.

### Inferences
- Pricing is **cap-bound**. Rp166 million (Java outside Jabodetabek) and Rp185 million (Bekasi/Jabodetabek) match the government's subsidized-house price caps. This is based on the researcher's background knowledge of the Kepmen PUPR 689/KPTS/M/2023 caps and should be verified. Price is therefore **not** a differentiator against the Detik-listed competitors. The Rp168–174 million prices likely reflect "subsidi plus" or newer cap and spec variants (unverified).
- The real competitive moats are **scale** (multi-regency land bank, a claimed 10K units), **low entry cost** (DP Rp1–3.5 million), **estate amenities and security tech**, and a **multi-channel sales machine** (in-house marketing plus a partner/agent portal plus portals). Most small competitors in the Detik list are single-project developers. This inference rests on project counts that are developer claims.
- The flood and permit controversies directly undercut the "quality / tahan lama / siap huni" positioning in the core Cirebon market.

### Gaps
- No independent sales-volume data (e.g., BP Tapera / FLPP disbursement by developer) was found to verify the "10,000 units" or "largest in Cirebon" claims.
- I did not obtain a competitor price list for "subsidi plus" products.

## 5. Digital strategy and how it compares with typical Indonesian developer tools

### Takeaway
Rumah Ningrat runs an **unusually broad digital stack for a regional subsidized developer**:
1. A marketing website with a WordPress SEO blog, an online booking flow and a self-booking PDF guide.
2. A **partner/agent portal (mitra.rumahningrat.com)** with a catalog, KPR simulator and attributed booking links.
3. A **buyer/resident dashboard (customer.rumahningrat.com, "Dashboard Warga Ningrat")** keyed to the buyer's KTP number, with After-Sales, **Cek Status KPR** and **Pengajuan Komplain** tabs.
4. A **resident app "Keluarga Ningrat"** with live CCTV, an SOS button, resident services and a marketplace.
5. Heavy short-video marketing on TikTok and YouTube, plus Facebook and Instagram, and WhatsApp as the primary contact channel.

Compared with off-the-shelf Indonesian developer software and the user's planned OS, the public-facing gaps are an **in-house installment, late-fee and accounting layer**, which matters less for a bank-KPR/FLPP developer, a **public interactive site plan / unit availability**, **OTP-based buyer login**, and evidence of **automated WhatsApp notifications** and complaint SLAs. The Klayan 5 escalation suggests the complaint tab has not prevented disputes from going public.

### Inferences
- **Notable features** that the user should benchmark against are:
  - a buyer-facing **KPR status tracker**, which is very relevant for FLPP developers where the bank and SiKasep pipeline is the buyer's biggest anxiety;
  - a **complaint/after-sales tab** inside the buyer dashboard;
  - a **partner portal with attributed booking links** (agent and affiliate networks drive subsidized-housing sales);
  - a **post-handover resident app** (CCTV, SOS, marketplace), which extends the relationship and doubles as a marketing USP.
- **Likely gaps / opportunities** relative to the user's product:
  - (a) No visible in-house installment, late-fee or accounting-journal layer. For FLPP developers that is mostly DP installments and booking fees, but for in-house or syariah developers it is core, so the user's OS targets a different or broader need.
  - (b) No visible structured cancellation/refund workflow.
  - (c) KTP-number-keyed access, with no OTP visible, is weaker identity verification than OTP. That matters under the UU PDP data-protection law because the KTP number is a widely known identifier. This is an inference, not a confirmed vulnerability.
  - (d) No public interactive site plan on the brand's own site.
  - (e) No evidence of SLA, escalation or construction-defect tracking. The Klayan 5 case (flooding since 2023, ending in petitions and DPRD hearings) shows the business value of complaint tracking with escalation, PSU-handover status and site-plan elevation/QC records, which the user's OS could add as differentiators.
- The domain sprawl (5+ domains and subdomains, a placeholder "test" award and a "2028" typo) suggests fast, marketing-led iteration and some content-governance weakness rather than a single integrated platform.

### Gaps
- I could not see the authenticated features of the customer dashboard, the mitra portal or the Keluarga Ningrat app: whether OTP exists, payment history, document vault, notification channels, and complaint SLA.
- No data on ad spend (Meta Ad Library / TikTok Ads) was retrieved.
- Commission structure for mitra/agents is not published.
- App ratings and reviews were not examined (covered by other researchers).
