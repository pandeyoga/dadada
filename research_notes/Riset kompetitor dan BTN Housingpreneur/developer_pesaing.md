## Q1. Which subsidized/affordable developers operate at scale (multi-project, multi-city), and which have their own apps or portals?

### Takeaway
The biggest subsidized (FLPP) players found are Vista Land Group (more than 60,000 units handed over, 30+ projects), Perumnas (state-owned, more than 500,000 units since 1974), SPS Group / Sri Pertiwi Sejati (about 15,000 subsidized units sold in 2017), and Pesona Kahuripan Group (about 2,400 to 4,000+ units a year). None of them has a resident super-app like Keluarga Ningrat. Perumnas has the most developed buyer and affiliate stack (Blibli store, a Digital Property Platform with a KPR simulator, and the "Sobat Bisnis Perumnas" affiliate scheme). Among subsidized developers, the only own-brand resident app found was **Rumahku Puri Delta Asri** (Kendal, 500+ installs). Pesona Kahuripan has a small booking/KPR app that has not been updated since July 2024.

### Inferences
- Among subsidized developers found, the scale leaders (Vista Land, SPS, Pesona Kahuripan) sell through WhatsApp, offline marketing, mass akad events and bank tools rather than their own apps. Volume does not seem to depend on owning a digital stack in this segment.
- Rumah Ningrat's claimed about 10,000 units sold puts it in the same order of magnitude as Pesona Kahuripan's annual run-rate. It is well below Vista Land's cumulative 60,000+, and it is concentrated in one region (Cirebon). That makes Pesona Kahuripan, Vista Land and the BP Tapera ">1,500 unit" band winners the closest peers by scale.
- Perumnas is the only subsidized-segment player found with an affiliate scheme comparable in concept to Mitra Ningrat.

### Gaps
- No public ranking of individual developers by 2025 FLPP volume was found. BP Tapera publishes association-level totals, and its awards use bands without unit counts. A Tempo article titled "BP Tapera Ungkap Sejumlah Pengembang Besar di Rumah Subsidi FLPP" ([Tempo](https://www.tempo.co/ekonomi/bp-tapera-ungkap-sejumlah-pengembang-besar-di-rumah-subsidi-flpp-2025562)) was blocked by Cloudflare, so its contents are unknown.
- SPS Group's current (2025–2026) volumes and its corporate website were not found. All SPS scale data is from 2017–2018.
- BTN/Tapera winners such as Bangun Famili Sejahtera, Shifa Isthin Neisya, Karya Mandiri Propertindo Utama and Bestow Harapan Indah were not checked individually beyond store searches, for lack of time.

## Q2. Profiles of the closest subsidized/affordable comparables and their digital stacks

### Takeaway
Of the subsidized/affordable peers profiled:
- **Perumnas** is closest to Rumah Ningrat on the sales side: online store (Blibli), KPR simulation through to unit booking, a digital platform with an affiliate community, and about 60% of sales attributed to digital content. It has no resident app.
- **Puri Delta Asri (Kendal)** is closest on the resident side, with billing/iuran status, announcements and service letters.
- **Pesona Kahuripan** has a website KPR simulator and a small, stale booking/KPR app.
- **Vista Land, SPS, Infiniti and Delta Group** show no own apps.
- No peer shows live CCTV, a geofenced gate, SOS, PPOB/marketplace, a buyer dashboard with construction progress, or a tiered-commission agent portal with leaderboards.

Rumah Ningrat's buyer dashboard (KPR status, construction progress) is therefore a differentiator on the consumer side, not on the back office.
- The low install counts of peer apps (100+ to 500+) suggest these are early-stage or single-estate tools.

## Q3. Reference large commercial developers: what their resident and buyer apps do

### Takeaway
The large developers all run estate or resident apps (IPL and utility billing, permits, complaints, SOS, visitor access, facility booking). Two also run sales or agent apps: Sinar Mas Land's eAgent and Paramount Sales. Public ratings are mostly poor (iOS 1.4–2.9★), which shows that resident apps are hard to execute well even with large budgets. None combines resident, buyer-progress and agent functions in one ecosystem in the way Rumah Ningrat describes.

## Q5. Gaps: which digital capabilities are common vs rare among subsidized developers?

### Takeaway
Among subsidized developers, what is common is marketing-layer tooling: website catalogs, WhatsApp, social media, occasional KPR calculators, and bank or government back-office tools (BTN Properti for Developer, SiKumbang/SiKasep). Rare or absent:
- resident apps with payments, CCTV, gate access or SOS (only Rumah Ningrat; partially Puri Delta Asri)
- buyer dashboards showing KPR status and construction progress (none found)
- structured agent/affiliate portals (only Perumnas's new affiliate feature)
- online self-booking (Perumnas via Blibli; Pesona Kahuripan's stale app)

### Inferences
- **Rare among subsidized developers, and so Rumah Ningrat's clearest differentiators:**
  1. An in-estate security stack in the app (live CCTV, geofenced gate, SOS).
  2. A buyer-facing construction and KPR progress dashboard.
  3. An agent portal with tiered commissions, personal landing pages and a leaderboard.
  4. Household services (PPOB and marketplace) in one resident app.
- **Most likely to be copied soon:** affiliate programs (Perumnas has started) and resident billing apps (Puri Delta Asri, plus cheap white-label options such as RT Pintar and Klasterin).
- Big-developer apps rate poorly (iOS 1.4–2.9★), which suggests reliability and service follow-through matter more than feature count. That is a risk for any feature-rich resident app in the MBR segment.
- Government consolidation (rumah.go.id, SiKasep) may commoditize KPR-status visibility over time, which could reduce the uniqueness of a developer-run KPR tracker.

### Gaps
- No feature-level hands-on tests were done (no logins). Features are as described in store listings, websites and press.
- Agent commission levels at subsidized developers (other than third-party portal claims) were not found.
- Install figures are Play bands, and iOS rating counts are small. No download-trend data was available.
